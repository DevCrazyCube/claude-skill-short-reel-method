# Truths: moments this audience has lived

Raw material for step 1. Copy this file into your project and fill it in. A moment here is not a pitch: it still
needs a turn you can see, and it has to pass the pitch test and the cold read.

Only the "Fresher" table opens step 1. A worn-out moment needs its new angle written down first. Nothing from
"Stay away from" is ever used.

**Audience:** who they are, their age range, where they live, what they have in common.
**Collected:** the date, and where from. Note what you could not read (closed platforms, old sources).

## Stay away from

Say why at the top: who is in the audience (are there minors?), who the account speaks for, which advertising
rules apply where you post.

- Topics that harm or exclude part of the audience.
- Topics the law or the platform restricts for this audience.
- Real, named people and organisations.
